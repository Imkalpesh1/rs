Diversity, Equity and Belonging

At R Systems, we empower healthcare providers with cutting-edge digital health engineering, AI diagnostics, remote patient monitoring, and EHR integration—driving exceptional care outcomes while optimizing operational costs.

• CULTURAL CHANGE

Our commitment to cultural change

At RSi we are committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace where every individual is respected, valued, and empowered. We recognize that diversity enhances creativity, innovation, and collaboration, which are essential for our success. Our strategy is rooted in our core values and built on four key pillars, aiming to create an environment with diverse perspectives, inclusive practices, and equitable opportunities that can also contribute to a better society.

Inclusive Leadership

Our leaders are committed to modeling inclusive behaviors and creating an environment where all voices are heard. We aim to inspire our teams to embrace diversity and leverage different viewpoints for innovative problem-solving.

Inclusive Teams

We build inclusive teams that reflect a wide range of perspectives, experiences, and talents. We promote a culture of belonging where team members feel safe to express their ideas and challenge the status quo.

Equitable Processes

We ensure that all employees have equal access to opportunities, resources, and support. This includes transparent hiring practices, equitable performance evaluations, and accessible career development programs.

DEI Enablement

We provide comprehensive training programs, workshops, and resources that empower employees to be advocates for DEI. Our internal communications celebrate diversity and share best practices, encouraging employees to take an active role in fostering an inclusive culture.

• OUR VISION & COMMITMENT

We believe that our strength comes from the diversity of thought and experience each team member brings to our global community. By fostering a culture that values diverse perspectives, we can engage with a variety of viewpoints and integrate them to support our mission. Diversity, equity, and inclusion aren't just values-they're essential to our creativity and success. This mindset helps us see the world from the perspectives of our stakeholders and adapt to its ongoing changes.

We are committed to creating an environment where every voice is valued and respected. By embracing our differences, promoting fairness, and fostering belonging, we grow together and build a brighter future. Embracing diverse thinking not only helps us understand our business environment better but also strengthens our ability to execute our global strategy. Together, we're more creative and better prepared to succeed globally.

Raluca Rusu
CEO, R Systems Europe
